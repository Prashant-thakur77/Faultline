# ADR-004: Order confirmation email is best-effort

Checkout places the order and charges the customer before it asks the email service to send a
confirmation. A failure or timeout in that call is logged and retried asynchronously; it never fails
the order and never holds the checkout response open.

We chose this because a blocked confirmation email had previously turned a minor mail-provider slowdown
into failed orders. Revenue-critical work stays on the synchronous path; notifications do not. Any change
that makes the email call blocking (`checkout.email.blocking: true`) needs a new decision.
