# ADR-013: Payment client keeps at least 2 retries

Charges are idempotent on the processor side (each carries an idempotency key), so the payment client
retries a failed or timed-out charge at least twice with jittered backoff before reporting failure to
checkout.

This pairs with ADR-012: short timeouts are only safe because transient processor errors are retried.
Removing retries, or dropping below two, needs a new decision.
