# ADR-012: Payment client timeout must stay at or below 2000 ms

The payment client's call timeout to the card processor is capped at 2000 ms. Checkout holds a
connection for every in-flight charge, so a longer timeout turns a slow processor into connection-pool
exhaustion in checkout and failed orders across the storefront.

When the processor is slow we prefer a fast, retryable failure (see ADR-013) over a long wait. Raising
the timeout above 2000 ms requires a new decision with a capacity review of checkout.
