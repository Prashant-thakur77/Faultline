## What didn't work / limits (derived from this run)

- Warm memory did not reduce tool calls for the deterministic investigator: it measures every suspect anyway. Memory changes ordering and the plan's evidence, not the amount of work here.
- The naive recency baseline is not useless: top-1 0.1 (ties scored 1/k). It cannot abstain, so it blames a change on every control (abstention 0.0).
- Memory off (A2) and on (A3) reach the same root causes on these scenarios: the gate decides from fresh evidence, by design. Memory's effect is in ordering, runbooks and the plan.
- Preflight backtest (acme): the gate flags 0.6525 of all historical PRs to catch 0.5455 of the incident-causing ones (precision 0.0603 vs base rate 0.0721): a real alert-fatigue cost at the current thresholds; at this threshold flagging is no better than flagging at random.
- Preflight backtest (acme): structure alone barely ranks causing PRs above the rest (AUROC 0.4255; with past incidents 0.5179). Most of the separation comes from the incident-memory term, which is partly self-fulfilling on this synthetic history.
- Preflight backtest (acme): nothing historical is blocked; without ChangeFacts the decision term cannot fire, and the other terms alone stay under the block threshold.
- Preflight backtest (globex): the gate flags 0.4804 of all historical PRs to catch 0.875 of the incident-causing ones (precision 0.1806 vs base rate 0.0992): a real alert-fatigue cost at the current thresholds.
- Preflight backtest (globex): structure alone barely ranks causing PRs above the rest (AUROC 0.6335; with past incidents 0.8075). Most of the separation comes from the incident-memory term, which is partly self-fulfilling on this synthetic history.
- Preflight backtest: the as-of bus factor counts only earlier commits, but a person with no team membership history (executives) keeps their current active flag, a small read of the present.
- Scale benchmark: reads that hit their template LIMIT (rows beyond it are not seen): Q-BLAST-1 (hub), Q-BLAST-2 (hub), Q-CUT-2 (hub). Top-k reads by design (Q-CHOKE-1, Q-CHOKE-ALL, Q-ORG-HARM, Q-RANK-1: highest scores first) are not listed.
- Robustness breaks on `missing` at its first setting 0.2: correct 83/100 (from 100), wrong-confident 0, abstained 37.
- The pre-registered ladder expectation was wrong: removing `algo.maxFlow` or `algo.SPpaths` did not raise wrong-confident blames (see the ladder table for where the strict-grade misses went).
- The held-out gate bench has no cause two or more hops from its symptom (the deep bench covers 1–4 hops, in the same simulator).
- Correct target is graded only where the plan offered an enabled production-changing fix (A3: 0.8 of graded trials).
- Trials are deterministic: N = 3 checks reproducibility, not statistical variance. All scenarios and brain questions are self-authored.
